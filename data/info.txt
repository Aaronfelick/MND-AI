SwiftFreight UAE

Company:
SwiftFreight UAE is a full-service freight and logistics company based in Dubai.

Services:
- Door-to-door delivery within the UAE and internationally.
- Sea freight: FCL full container and LCL shared container.
- Air freight: express and economy.
- Warehousing and storage in Dubai and Sharjah.
- Customs clearance and documentation.
- Last-mile delivery across all UAE emirates.

General pricing guidance when no exact spreadsheet rate is available:
- UAE domestic: 3-5 AED per kg, minimum charge 25 AED.
- GCC countries: 10-18 AED per kg for Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Qatar, and Saudi Arabia.
- International air economy: from 25 AED per kg.
- International air express: from 55 AED per kg.
- Sea freight LCL: from 180 AED per CBM.
- Warehousing: 2 AED per pallet per day.

Transit times:
- Same emirate: same day or next day.
- Different UAE emirate: 1-2 business days.
- GCC destinations including Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Kuwait, and Bahrain: 2-4 business days.
- Europe including UK, Germany, and France: 5-7 days by air or 25-35 days by sea.
- USA: 7-10 days by air or 30-40 days by sea.
- Asia including India, China, and Pakistan: 3-5 days by air or 18-25 days by sea.

Restricted items:
- Firearms and weapons.
- Narcotics and illegal substances.
- Live animals.
- Perishable food unless special arrangement is made.

Special handling:
- Fragile packaging: 15 AED per item.
- Maximum single parcel: 500 kg. Heavier cargo needs a custom quote.

Every shipment gets a tracking ID. Customers can track at swiftfreight.ae or send the tracking ID on WhatsApp for real-time updates.

Payment:
- Cash.
- Credit or debit card.
- Bank transfer.
- Business accounts: 30-day credit terms available by email application.

FAQs:
- Can I ship a car or motorbike? Yes. Contact SwiftFreight for a custom quote based on destination and vehicle size.
- Do you offer cargo insurance? Yes. Ask when booking for details and pricing.
- What if my package is damaged? Contact support@swiftfreight.ae immediately with photos. SwiftFreight has a claims process.
- Do you pick up from my location? Yes. Door-to-door pickup is included within the UAE.
