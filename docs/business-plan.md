# MND-AI business plan

## 1. What you are building

MND-AI is a productized service for local businesses that already have websites but are weak in two places:

1. They do not appear clearly in AI-powered search answers.
2. They lose website visitors because nobody helps the visitor book, ask questions, or choose the right service.

Your offer solves both:

- **AEO consultancy:** structure the website so answer engines can understand the business and surface it for local intent queries.
- **Hyper-local concierge bot:** deploy a website chatbot trained only on approved business information that answers common questions and routes visitors to booking, WhatsApp, phone, or forms.

## 2. Ideal customer profile

Start with businesses that:

- already have a functioning website
- rely on local trust and local discovery
- get repeated customer questions
- depend on booking, appointments, or quote requests
- do not have a high-quality chatbot or structured schema setup

Best first verticals:

- dental and aesthetic clinics
- physiotherapy clinics
- gyms and personal trainers
- salons and spas
- tailoring and alterations shops
- cleaning services
- home maintenance businesses
- nurseries and education centers
- cafes and specialty food businesses

## 3. Core customer pain points

### Discovery pain

- Their site is not structured for AI search systems.
- Their services are not clearly mapped in schema.
- Their FAQs are weak or missing.
- Their trust signals are buried in random pages or social posts.
- Their location and service-area data is inconsistent.

### Conversion pain

- Site visitors have simple questions and leave without converting.
- Nobody captures after-hours leads.
- Important booking or inquiry flows are unclear.
- Staff waste time answering repetitive phone and WhatsApp questions.

## 4. Productized offers

### Offer 1: AEO Foundation

Deliverables:

- website/page audit
- service and entity mapping
- organization, local business, service, and FAQ schema
- AEO question map
- FAQ rewrite
- trust-signal extraction
- implementation guidance for homepage, service pages, and location pages

Best for:

- businesses with some traffic but weak AI-search visibility

### Offer 2: Website Concierge Bot

Deliverables:

- business knowledge base
- system prompt and guardrails
- lead-capture flow
- handoff rules to WhatsApp, phone, booking page, or staff
- suggested questions for the widget
- deployment copy and testing checklist

Best for:

- businesses with recurring pre-sales questions

### Offer 3: AEO + Concierge Growth Stack

Deliverables:

- everything in Offer 1 and Offer 2
- monthly FAQ refreshes
- new service-page answer packs
- analytics review
- bot tuning based on user conversations
- quarterly trust-signal refresh

Best for:

- businesses that want an ongoing managed service

## 5. Recommended service workflow

### Step 1: Intake

Collect:

- website URL
- business category
- services
- service areas
- languages
- contact details
- booking path
- trust signals
- top 10 repeated customer questions

### Step 2: Data normalization

Convert raw information into a structured business profile JSON document.

That document becomes the source for:

- structured data
- AEO question maps
- FAQ content
- chatbot knowledge
- lead handoff config

### Step 3: AEO asset production

Generate:

- Organization schema
- LocalBusiness schema
- Service schema
- FAQPage schema
- answer-engine brief
- priority AI-search questions

### Step 4: Chatbot asset production

Generate:

- chatbot system prompt
- knowledge base
- suggested questions
- lead capture fields
- escalation rules

### Step 5: Implementation

Either:

- hand implementation docs to the client's web team, or
- install the schema and chatbot for them directly

### Step 6: Measurement

Track:

- leads captured from chat
- appointment requests
- FAQ deflection
- pages with richer AI-search coverage
- branded and non-branded discovery improvements

## 6. How to position this in sales calls

Use simple language. Do not lead with jargon.

Bad pitch:

> We optimize your answer engine presence via semantic entity mapping.

Better pitch:

> When someone asks an AI assistant where to find a trusted clinic, gym, tailor, or cafe near them, your business should be easy to understand and easy to recommend. We make that happen, and we also add a website assistant that helps visitors book instead of bouncing.

## 7. Suggested discovery questions

Ask prospects:

1. What are the top 10 questions customers ask before they book?
2. Do you currently capture after-hours website leads?
3. Do visitors mostly call, WhatsApp, or book online?
4. Which service is most profitable?
5. Which location or service do you want to grow first?
6. Are your reviews and trust credentials clearly visible on the website?
7. If someone asks ChatGPT or Google AI about your business type in your city, what answer do you want to appear?

## 8. Pilot plan

Start with one vertical and one city cluster.

Example:

- tailors and alterations in Sharjah
- dental clinics in Dubai
- boutique gyms in Abu Dhabi

Why this helps:

- the intake questions repeat
- service structures repeat
- FAQ patterns repeat
- you can turn delivery into templates quickly

## 9. What success looks like

For the client:

- better site clarity

- faster answers to common questions
- better conversion from existing website traffic

For MND-AI:

- reusable onboarding template
- reusable JSON schema generator workflow
- reusable chatbot prompt patterns
- vertical-specific sales playbooks
